How to determine your correct ski length​

​When choosing the right ski length, there are a couple of factors you need to take into consideration. The most important are your height, weight, ability level, and the type of skiing you plan to do.​

​As a general guideline, choose a ski that reaches somewhere between your chin and the top of your head (or, for some skis, slightly above your head). A shorter ski makes shorter turns and is typically easier to maneuver at lower speeds, while a longer ski will have a longer turn radius and generally offers greater stability and confidence at higher speeds.​

​Taller, heavier, and more advanced skiers often prefer slightly longer skis because they have more leverage over the ski. Shorter, lighter people and less-experienced skiers will usually feel more comfortable with the agility of a shorter ski.​

​The recommendations below provide a general starting point for choosing the right ski length within Atomic's ski range. Individual preferences, ski models, and intended terrain may influence the ideal length.​

​All Mountain​

All-mountain skis are designed to perform across a wide variety of conditions, so the ideal length depends on where you ski most often. A ski around your height is a good starting point. If you spend more time on groomed slopes or in tighter terrain, a slightly shorter ski (minus 5 to 10 cm) will feel more agile. If you frequently explore ungroomed terrain, softer snow, or wide-open slopes, a slightly longer ski (plus 5 to 10 cm) can provide greater stability and floatation.​

About

Built for every style from freeride to freestyle, the wildly versatile Atomic Bent 100 is a do-everything ski. Rooted in freeride with a side of all-mountain and a dash of art by Chris Benchetler, the celebrated Bent 100 has been reimagined from tip to tail. Incorporating performance feedback from Atomic athletes, the Bent 100 features a Light Woodcore and Powder Rocker with 5% more rocker for add tail pop and easier transitioning to switch. Freeride specific HRZN 3D delivers improved float in deeper snow and better handling in variable conditions. Dura Cap Sidewalls offer controlled power transmission and commanding edge grip on harder snow and a thicker Resist Edge adds greater durability for sliding park rails. For Bobby Brown it’s a no brainer, the Bent 100 is his everything ski. Playful and sturdy, this is the one that does it all.
